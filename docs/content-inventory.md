## 2. Proposed Core Service Lines (Draft for Client Sign-Off)

### 1. Drilling Support Services
- **Summary:** Operational coordination and rig-site technical support for safe drilling programmes.
- **Overview:** Meddy Oil & Gas delivers specialised support for drilling operations across onshore and offshore environments. Our team coordinates rig-site operations, drilling fluids logistics, and equipment readiness to maintain continuous uptime and adhere strictly to operator safety standards.
- **Coverage:**
  * Drilling programme coordination and site planning
  * Rig-site mud logging and fluids monitoring support
  * Well control contingency and equipment verification
  * Daily drilling reporting and technical logging

### 2. Well Construction & Completions
- **Summary:** Tubular running, casing support, and completion equipment installation.
- **Overview:** We support operators during critical well construction phases. From casing handling to completion hardware installation, our technicians ensure mechanical integrity and tight adherence to well design tolerances.
- **Coverage:**
  * Casing and tubing running support
  * Liner hanger installation supervision
  * Wellhead and Christmas tree assembly support
  * Downhole completion tool handling

### 3. Wireline & Well Intervention
- **Summary:** Electric line, slickline, and mechanical intervention for production optimization.
- **Overview:** We provide intervention support for both cased hole and open hole environments. Our focus is on diagnostic logging, mechanical repairs, and zone isolation to safeguard reservoir performance.
- **Coverage:**
  * Slickline mechanical operations and valve servicing
  * Cased hole logging and perforation support
  * Tubing punch and plug setting services
  * Pressure control equipment setup and testing

### 4. Engineering & Technical Services
- **Summary:** Field design, maintenance planning, asset integrity, and inspection.
- **Overview:** Meddy provides multi-disciplinary engineering support to assist asset operators. We help plan preventative maintenance, inspect structural assets, and provide technical evaluations to extend production lifespan.
- **Coverage:**
  * Structural and piping integrity inspection
  * Preventative maintenance scheduling
  * Process engineering reviews
  * As-built documentation and regulatory reporting

### 5. Procurement & Supply Chain
- **Summary:** Sourcing, testing, and delivery of API-certified equipment and consumables.
- **Overview:** We manage the procurement and logistics chain for oilfield tools, pipework, valves, and specialized consumables. Every item is verified for certification and material trace before delivery to the field.
- **Coverage:**
  * OCTG tubulars and casing accessories
  * High-pressure valves, flanges, and fittings
  * Rig consumables and safety gear
  * Expedited customs and local logistics coordination

### 6. Pipeline & Facilities Support
- **Summary:** Construction monitoring, pipeline inspection, and station maintenance.
- **Overview:** We support operators in maintaining pipeline corridors and surface processing facilities. Our technicians assist with non-destructive testing, corrosion monitoring, and routine mechanical maintenance.
- **Coverage:**
  * Pipeline right-of-way surveillance and inspection
  * Non-destructive testing (NDT) and wall thickness checks
  * Surface facility valve and pump maintenance
  * Flange torqueing and hydrostatic testing support
